Amna Ahmad respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected in connection with this SMS messaging service.

Information Collected

Information may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Information provided through an inquiry
  • SMS messages and related communication data

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Send SMS messages related to an inquiry
  • Test and operate the messaging service
  • Provide support
  • Maintain and improve the service

SMS Messaging

SMS messages are sent only to individuals who have consented to receive them. During development and testing, messaging may be limited to authorized test recipients.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS consent and mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate and deliver the messaging service.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
